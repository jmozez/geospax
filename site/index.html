How it works

From raw data to a finished map in four steps.

  1. 01

    Bring data

    Drop files, plug in a device, or fetch open data for the area on the map.

  2. 02

    It styles itself

    Colours, classes and labels chosen for what the data is, with the reason shown.

  3. 03

    Find patterns

    Hot spots, terrain, catchments, networks and classification in a click.

  4. 04

    Finish

    Layout, PNG, PDF, SVG and a Word report, ready to send.

Print-quality 3D

Mount Wilhelm, 4,509 m.

The Copernicus 30 m elevation model with satellite imagery placed pixel by pixel, ray-traced shadows and ambient occlusion, rendered in R with rayshader. One analysis, no code.

  • Imagery warped onto the DEM's own grid
  • Title, north arrow, size and sources on the picture
  • Flat shaded relief and a report alongside
