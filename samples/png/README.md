# Papua New Guinea sample data (from GeoSpaX 1.4.2)

Open it from **Data › Sample data**; GeoSpaX copies it into your data folder (`Samples/png/`) and adds
every layer to the map.

| File | What it is | Source and licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `species_richness_transects.geojson` | 42 survey points on two montane transects (YUS, Huon Peninsula; Mt Wilhelm, Chimbu): elevation, species richness, tree cover, rainfall | **Fictional** but ecologically realistic (mid-elevation richness peak); in4metrix, GeoSpaX 1.4.2, MIT |
| `birdwing_butterfly_presences.geojson` | 40 presence points of *Ornithoptera priamus* with counts and elevation | Teaching data, in4metrix, GeoSpaX 1.4.2, MIT |
| `birdwing_host_vines.geojson` | 53 points of the host vine *Aristolochia tagala*: density, canopy | Teaching data, in4metrix, GeoSpaX 1.4.2, MIT |
| `forest_patches.geojson` | 35 forest patch polygons: area, connectivity, disturbance, protection | Simulated teaching data, GeoSpaX 1.4.2, MIT |
| `habitat_survey_grid.geojson` | 90-cell survey grid: elevation, rainfall, NDVI, mean temperature, habitat type, canopy | Simulated teaching data, GeoSpaX 1.4.2, MIT |
| `stream_water_sources.geojson` | 28 stream points: order, flow, distance to road | Simulated teaching data, GeoSpaX 1.4.2, MIT |
| `worldclim_bio1_temperature.tif` | BIO1 annual mean temperature, °C, 30″ (~1 km) | WorldClim 2.1, Fick & Hijmans (2017) *Int. J. Climatol.* 37: 4302–4315; CC BY 4.0 |
| `worldclim_bio12_precipitation.tif` | BIO12 annual precipitation, mm, 30″ | WorldClim 2.1 (as above), CC BY 4.0 |
| `tree_cover_pct.tif` | Tree cover, % of each 30″ cell | Derived from ESA WorldCover 10 m 2021 v200 (Zanaga et al. 2022, doi:10.5281/zenodo.7254221), CC BY 4.0 |

**Not included** (licence): the 1.4.2 province boundaries (source not recorded), the WDPA protected areas
(the WDPA terms do not allow redistribution; Data › Online data has boundaries, and Protected Planet
offers the WDPA to download yourself) and the 128 GBIF Emperor bird-of-paradise records (each GBIF record
carries its own licence; the species distribution kit downloads current records with their citation).

The fictional and simulated layers are for learning the tools, not for conclusions about Papua New Guinea.
